The primary, comprehensive assumptions register is maintained in [`docs/ASSUMPTIONS.md`](file:///Volumes/SHIV%20BHALKE/TECHMASTER/USEDBOOK%20PROJECT/docs/ASSUMPTIONS.md).

Please refer to that file for full details on:
1. **Taxation & GST Regulations (India)** (HSN 4901 for books, 18% GST on commissions, TCS under Section 52)
2. **Payments & Escrow Settlements** (RBI PA/PG compliance, nodal routing, T+3 settlement window)
3. **Logistics & Courier Rates** (Bidar 585401/585403, Volumetric weight calculations, RTO handling)
4. **Condition Grading & Quality Governance** (Strict defect disclosure, anti-piracy policies)

All unverified commercial rules are explicitly flagged with `REQUIRES_VERIFICATION` or `CONFIGURABLE`.
